LESSON 01 · MARKET STRUCTURE

Bullish and Bearish Structure

An uptrend generally forms higher highs (HH) and higher lows (HL). A downtrend generally forms lower lows (LL) and lower highs (LH).

LESSON 02 · BREAK OF STRUCTURE

BOS – Break of Structure

A bullish BOS occurs when price breaks a significant previous swing high. A bearish BOS occurs when price breaks a significant swing low. Look for a meaningful candle close beyond the level.

LESSON 03 · CHANGE OF CHARACTER

CHoCH – Possible Market Shift

A bullish CHoCH can occur when price breaks a meaningful lower high during a downtrend. It suggests a possible change in direction, not guaranteed reversal.

LESSON 04 · LIQUIDITY

Liquidity Sweep

Price moves above a previous high, where buy-side liquidity may sit, then rejects back below. A sweep needs further confirmation before becoming a trading setup.

LESSON 05 · ORDER BLOCK

Order Blocks

A bullish order block is commonly studied around the last bearish candle before strong bullish displacement. Traders examine the context and structure break before considering the zone.

LESSON 06 · FAIR VALUE GAP

FVG – Fair Value Gap

A bullish FVG is the imbalance between candle one's high and candle three's low when they do not overlap. A bearish FVG is the opposite. Price may revisit these zones, but a fill is not guaranteed.
