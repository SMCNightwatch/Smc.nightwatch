LESSON 01 · BEGINNER

Market Structure

Learn how price moves through trends.

Uptrend: Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL).
Downtrend: Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL).
LESSON 02 · BEGINNER

Break of Structure (BOS)

BOS occurs when price breaks a significant swing point in the direction of the existing trend.

Remember: Look for a meaningful break, preferably confirmed by a candle close.
LESSON 03 · INTERMEDIATE

Change of Character (CHoCH)

A potential early signal that the existing market direction may be changing.

Remember: Wait for further confirmation before taking a trade.
LESSON 04 · INTERMEDIATE

Liquidity

Liquidity often gathers around equal highs, equal lows and obvious swing points.

Study: Buy-side liquidity (BSL) and sell-side liquidity (SSL).
LESSON 05 · INTERMEDIATE

Liquidity Sweep

Price moves beyond a liquidity level and then rejects or returns back through it.

Study: Watch for rejection and structure confirmation. A sweep alone is not an entry signal.
LESSON 06 · ADVANCED

Order Blocks

Study potential reaction zones around the last opposing candle before a strong price move.

Study: Look for displacement, structure breaks and a return to the zone.
LESSON 07 · ADVANCED

Fair Value Gaps (FVG)

An imbalance between three candles where price moves quickly through an area.

Study: Bullish FVG and bearish FVG, and how price reacts when revisiting them.
LESSON 08 · ADVANCED

Multi-Timeframe Analysis

Combine higher and lower timeframes to build a structured trading idea.

Example: Daily or 4H bias, 1H setup, 15M confirmation.
