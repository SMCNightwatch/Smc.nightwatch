Learn by doing

Turn chart theory into chart practice.

SMC Nightwatch is built around learning to think independently. Use marked-up examples, replay exercises and simple quizzes to test what you understand.

  • Identify swing highs and swing lows.
  • Mark a BOS or CHoCH and explain why.
  • Find a possible liquidity pool.
  • Write down entry, stop-loss and invalidation logic before considering a trade.

Our mission

Knowledge before execution.

SMC Nightwatch exists to make SMC concepts easier to understand through structured lessons, chart examples and a culture of patience. Our goal is to help learners build their own process — not depend on calls or promises.

Is SMC Nightwatch suitable for beginners?

Yes. Start with the foundation lessons and move through the learning path at your own pace. No prior SMC knowledge is required.

Does SMC guarantee profitable trades?

No. SMC is an analytical framework, not a guarantee. Trading involves substantial risk, and examples are educational rather than instructions to buy or sell.

Do I need to pay to learn here?

This starter website is designed as a free educational resource. Any future paid offering should be clearly described with its price and terms.
