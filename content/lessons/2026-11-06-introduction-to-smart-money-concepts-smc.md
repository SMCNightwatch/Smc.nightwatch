Introduction to Smart Money Concepts (SMC)



Welcome to SMC Nightwatch!



Smart Money Concepts (SMC) is a trading approach that studies how large market participants influence price movements.



What you will learn:



1. Market Structure

2. Break of Structure (BOS)

3. Change of Character (CHoCH)

4. Liquidity and Liquidity Sweeps

5. Fair Value Gaps (FVG)

6. Order Blocks

7. Entry, Stop Loss and Take Profit



Our goal



To help beginners understand the forex market, develop patience, manage risk and build trading discipline.
